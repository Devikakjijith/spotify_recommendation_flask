Performance Interpretation

  • Explained variance: Higher values mean more of the original data's variation is retained.
  • Reconstruction MSE: Lower values mean less reconstruction error.
  • Dimensionality reduction: Shows how much the number of features has decreased.
  • Silhouette score: Measures cluster separation. Values closer to 1 generally indicate better-separated clusters.

Silhouette score evaluates K-Means clustering on the reduced features, not PCA directly.
